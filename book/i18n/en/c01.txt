[116] Adobe and After Effects: 30 Years <span class="lv">●○○</span>
[117] After Effects began at a small company in 1993 and became the standard for motion graphics in film, broadcast and advertising. In 2026 it reached a third turning point, as AI arrived as a “colleague you work with”.
[118] 0-1 A Company Called Adobe
[119] Adobe was founded in 1982 by John Warnock and Charles Geschke. It started with PostScript, a page description language for printing, and with Photoshop in 1990 and Premiere in 1991 it grew from a “publishing company” into an “image and video company”. In 2013 it ended the boxed Creative Suite (CS) and moved to the monthly subscription Creative Cloud (CC), and in 2023 it launched the generative AI Firefly.
[120] <b>Why it matters</b>Adobe apps are connected. You send what you make in AE to Premiere and import Photoshop layers as they are. AI is arriving the same way, starting with “connecting the apps”.
[121] 0-2 The Birth of After Effects
[122] After Effects was made by CoSA (The Company of Science and Art), an American company founded in 1990. Version 1.0 came out in January 1993; by today’s standards it was very simple, with no Timeline panel and only one effect per layer. In July that year CoSA was bought by Aldus, and when Aldus merged with Adobe in 1994, AE became an Adobe product. The first version under the Adobe name was 3.0 in 1995, and it ran only on the Mac until 3.1 added Windows support in 1997.
[123] Until then, film visual effects were made by big studios with expensive hardware and in-house software. With AE, which layers and composites on a personal computer, small teams could make broadcast titles and commercial effects. In 2019 it received a Scientific and Technical Academy Award for this contribution.

[188] 0-7 What Is Higgsfield?
[189] Higgsfield is a generative AI platform that lets you use video, image and voice generation models from many companies in one place. You choose models such as Seedance, Kling, Veo and Nano Banana and pay with the same credits. On 27 May 2026 it released a plugin for Adobe, so you can generate, remove backgrounds, reframe and upscale on the timeline without switching to a browser. In July, a feature was added that lets AI such as Claude control AE directly through MCP. One installer adds it to both AE and Premiere.
[190] 0-8 What Is MCP and Why Does It Matter?
[191] MCP (Model Context Protocol) is a common standard that lets AI call features of other programs. Anthropic released it in 2024, and by 2026 it had become the de facto standard for connecting AI and creative tools. Think of it as a <b>USB port for AI</b>. Because the port shape is the same, AI such as Claude or ChatGPT plugs into AE, Blender or DaVinci Resolve in the same way.
