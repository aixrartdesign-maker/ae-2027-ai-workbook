[4016] Case 3. Vertical Shorts card
[4017] <span class="lbl">PROMPT · to Claude</span>A 1080×1920, 6-second Shorts composition "Claude사례3_쇼츠카드". A white card bounces up from the bottom of a purple background and stops; inside the card, a lime NEW tag, the title "애펙 2027 / AI와 30초 만에" (AE 2027 / in 30 seconds with AI), two lines of body text and a navy "자세히 보기 →" (learn more) button appear one after another. The button grows slightly once at 3.5 s and returns. Parent every element inside the card to the card.
[4018] <b>Figure 10-6</b> 0.35 s · 0.9 s · 1.6 s · 3 s. The card has only 3 position keys; the text and button inside follow their parent (the card) up.
[4019] <b>What we fixed</b>At first the title and body were made left-aligned, and the text stuck out past the card's left edge (a problem with where AE text anchors sit). We remade them center-aligned and, after parenting, entered positions in <b>card-relative coordinates</b> (title 0, −200 / body 0, 60). Parenting while the parent is moving shifts the child's position unexpectedly, so it's safest to parent before adding animation. In Shorts, app buttons overlap the top and bottom, so keep important text inside the central 70% of the screen.

[4023] <b>What we fixed</b>Credits are easier to read at a constant speed, so we <b>added no easing</b> to the position keys (linear). Auto-correcting easing with the finishing tool (finalize), as in the other cases, would turn the linear keys into smooth ones, so don't use it on credits. If any scenes were made with AI, get into the habit of saying so in the credits too (Ch. 29).
