[1830] 26-3 Adobe Certification
[1831] Adobe runs the <b>Adobe Certified Professional</b> credential. Pass an app exam (for After Effects, Visual Effects &amp; Motion Graphics) and you receive a digital badge. You can take it at authorized test centers or through your school, so ask your department office or an Adobe education partner. It doesn’t test AI features directly, but it is a good way to prove the fundamentals needed to fix AI-made results by hand.
