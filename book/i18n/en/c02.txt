[280] <span class="tag t2">Why?</span>
[281] AE is a program that “calculates the in-between once you set the start and end”. If you record (keyframe) left at 0 s and right at 2 s, AE fills in the 58 pictures in between. This is called <b>interpolation</b>. F9 changes how it fills them from “constant speed” to “slow–fast–slow”.
