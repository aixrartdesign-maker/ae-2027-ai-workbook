[1189] <span class="tag t1">Follow Along</span> 13-5 Seeing Space with 4 Camera Views
[1190] In 3D it is easy to misjudge distance from one view. Looking from the top, front and side at once, like architectural drawings, shows where each layer is right away.
[1191] Choose <b>4 Views</b> from the <b>Select View Layout</b> menu below the Composition panel (or <b>View → Switch View Layout → 4 Views</b>).
[1192] Click each pane and choose its view from the <b>3D View Popup</b> below. The defaults are Top · Front · Right · Active Camera.
[1193] Dragging a layer in the Top pane moves only depth (z); in the Front pane, only height (y). Always check the final picture in the Active Camera pane.
[1194] <b>If it worked</b>The four panes show the same scene from different directions, and a layer moved in one pane moves in the others too.
[1195] <b>If you are stuck</b>If all four panes show the same picture, the layer’s 3D switch is off. If it is too slow, keep only the Active Camera pane at Full resolution and the others at Quarter.
